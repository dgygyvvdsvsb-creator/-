# From Zero to Legend — publishing rules (from EP3 on)

- Platforms: TikTok + YouTube Shorts. Every episode must qualify for monetisation:
  - length 63–75 s (TikTok Creator Rewards needs > 60 s; Shorts allow up to 3 min).
- Every episode ends with a ~6 s end card:
  1. "YOUR VOTE DECIDES EP. N+1"
  2. Panel "DON'T MISS EP. N+1" + button tapped by Grandad's orange glove
     (TikTok: "+ FOLLOW" → "FOLLOWING"; YouTube: "SUBSCRIBE" with bell → "SUBSCRIBED")
  3. Two vote chips + "VOTE IN THE COMMENTS" + mock comment box
- Two exports per episode: `EP{N}_TikTok.mp4`, `EP{N}_YouTube_Shorts.mp4`.
- Audience: USA. Post at 22:00 Germany time (16:00 New York), or schedule 01:00–03:00 Germany for the US evening peak.
- Hashtags: #soccer #football #fromzerotolegend #pixelart #goalkeeper (Shorts: add #shorts).

## Hook (first 3.5 s) — retention target 15–20 s average watch
- Frame 0 is already action and full colour (no white flash, no grey/dim caption): e.g. the keeper frozen mid-dive, ball at the gloves, score bar 90+3'.
- Sound hit at 0.0 s; whoosh on every cut.
- A new shot every ≤0.55 s in the hook: mid-dive → snap-zoom on the rival → boot hits the ball, ball flies at the camera → 0.3 s montage of the stakes (Grandad, scouts' message, ambulance lights, hero's face).
- Hook caption fully readable on frame 0 (TikTok preview); key words take colour when spoken.
- Never reveal the outcome in the hook (no LEFT/RIGHT labels, no "wrong side" hints).
