# From Zero to Legend — publishing rules (from EP3 on)

- Platforms: TikTok + YouTube Shorts. Every episode must qualify for monetisation:
  - length 63–75 s (TikTok Creator Rewards needs > 60 s; Shorts allow up to 3 min).
- Every episode ends with a ~6 s end card:
  1. "YOUR VOTE DECIDES EP. N+1"
  2. Panel "DON'T MISS EP. N+1" + button tapped by Grandad's orange glove
     (TikTok: "+ FOLLOW" → "FOLLOWING"; YouTube: "SUBSCRIBE" with bell → "SUBSCRIBED")
  3. Two vote chips + "VOTE IN THE COMMENTS" + mock comment box
- Two exports per episode: `EP{N}_TikTok.mp4`, `EP{N}_YouTube_Shorts.mp4`.
- Audience: USA. Post at 22:00 Germany time (16:00 New York), or schedule 01:00–03:00 Germany for the US evening peak.
- Hashtags: #soccer #football #fromzerotolegend #pixelart #goalkeeper (Shorts: add #shorts).

## Hook (first 3.5 s) — retention target 15–20 s average watch
- Frame 0 is already action and full colour (no white flash, no grey/dim caption): e.g. the keeper frozen mid-dive, ball at the gloves, score bar 90+3'.
- Sound hit at 0.0 s; whoosh on every cut.
- A new shot every ≤0.55 s in the hook: mid-dive → snap-zoom on the rival → boot hits the ball, ball flies at the camera → 0.3 s montage of the stakes (Grandad, scouts' message, ambulance lights, hero's face).
- Hook caption fully readable on frame 0 (TikTok preview); key words take colour when spoken.
- Never reveal the outcome in the hook (no LEFT/RIGHT labels, no "wrong side" hints).

## VISUAL STYLE — from EP5 on ONLY this style (user decision)
Reference: remi_sport "De zéro à champion du monde" EP7 (85 s).
- Characters = tiny pixel sprites (~18x30 px) with a 1-px dark outline and ONE shadow tone (right side),
  scaled up 6x–24x with NEAREST. Big in frame: mid shots ~1/4–1/2 of the width, close-ups fill the frame.
  Dives/falls = the sprite rotated with NEAREST (jagged edges are part of the look).
- Backgrounds simple, bright and smooth: sky gradient, blurred tree blobs, blurred pixel crowd, striped pitch,
  clean flat interiors (white walls, checker floor, a few props). Action > clutter.
- Football ACTION most of the time: matches, saves, tackles, injuries — diagonal compositions, camera close.
- FX on every hit: glow ring, flash, speed/impact lines, red flash for injuries, slow-mo.
- Pace: new shot every 2–3 s, caption chunk every ~1 s (Poppins ExtraBold ~50 px, white with thick outline,
  key words coloured, unspoken words dim).
- Story UI as devices: score bar ("1/4 2-0"), badges ("No 7 · 16"), white speech bubbles, scan screens,
  letters/notifications, recovery bars, typing box + two choice cards with icons at the end.
- Opening: previous vote result card (1 s) → straight into drama/action.
- Ending: life choice with risk, two cards + typing box, "vote in the comments", FOLLOW / SUBSCRIBE card.
- Our anchors stay: hero green #0 + red bracelet (right wrist) + Grandad's orange gloves; Grandad bald, white
  side hair, glasses, white beard, brown cardigan, cane; Mason blond spiky, red #9; banner "EP. N · FROM ZERO TO LEGEND".

## QA (user decision, saves tokens)
- Exactly ONE reviewer agent per episode, launched only after the full render.
  It does both jobs in one pass: frame-by-frame visuals (anatomy, scale, physics, round ball, keepers on the goal line,
  caption vs picture) AND story/continuity logic. Fix everything it finds, then at most one short re-check by the same agent.
- Goals are youth-size: height ≈ 1.2 × keeper height, width ≈ 2 × goal height. Show a small keeper by making HIM smaller, never the goal bigger.

## No text over characters (user rule, permanent)
- NO speech bubbles and NO name tags above characters. Dialogue appears ONLY in the captions.
- Keepers diving must land on the grass after the dive; catches = ball stays in the gloves.
- Objects passed between characters are held in their hands, never floating.

## TikTok retention rules (from EP6 on, user decision 2026-11-23)
Data (TikTok Studio, 237 views): ~45% of viewers leave in the first 2 s; after that the curve is almost flat
(the story holds), full-watch 7.3% at 75.7 s. YouTube Shorts holds ~24 s average and grows faster.
- TikTok layout: banner + captions BELOW TikTok's top UI (Following/For You tabs, LIVE, search):
  banner at y≈230, captions from y≈270 (of 1280). YouTube Shorts keeps the current layout.
- Frame 0: one big 3-5 word line in the upper half (not cut at the top), already moving, a sound hit;
  a question / shock / result-before-cause — never a recap like "You asked for…".
  The vote result comes at 2-3 s, not in the hook.
- TikTok cut 62-65 s (just over 60 s for rewards). YouTube may stay longer.
- Custom cover: 2-3 words in the CENTRE (the profile grid crops top and bottom).
- Every new episode: check the first 2 s on a phone-size preview with TikTok's UI zones drawn on top.
